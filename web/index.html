    How it runs, and what it can't do

    The loop

    A scheduled job fetches results, rebuilds two feature tables, retrains, predicts the coming round and commits. A second job scores the previous round. Nothing runs when you load this page — every number here was computed in CI and shipped as a static file.

    Honest limits

    • Market values are crowd estimates, not transfer fees. The model mostly learns that young players with minutes at big clubs cost more.
    • The style map has no ground truth. It is a visualisation, not a model with a score.
    • Draw prediction is close to unusable, which is the normal result and is shown above rather than hidden.
    • This is not a betting tool and carries no odds comparison.
